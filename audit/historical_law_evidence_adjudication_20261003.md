## Trust boundary

The new verification level is `evidence_adjudicated_historical_v1`.

It means:

- this was **not** Stage 6 high-confidence machine promotion;
- this is **not** labeled human/owner review;
- the decision is reproducible from tracked official question metadata, the selected official answer option, the exam-time MOJ article text/fingerprint, and the exact Stage 6 held state;
- Stage 6 thresholds remain unchanged;
- Official Core is never mutated or copied into the combined verified output.

`historical_law_evidence_adjudication.py` replays every decision and fails closed if the official answer, selected answer option evidence, question stem evidence, Stage 4 fingerprint/text, Stage 5 official-source links, Stage 6 held state, or selected version drifts.

A `held` adjudication is explicitly excluded from the combined verified result.
