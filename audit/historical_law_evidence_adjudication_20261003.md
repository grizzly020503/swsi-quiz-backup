# Historical Law Evidence Adjudication — 2026-10-03

## Purpose

Stage 6 correctly held six promotion candidates at `historical_semantic_support` because semantic confidence alone was not high enough for automatic promotion. This slice does **not** lower that threshold. It adds a separate, replayable evidence-adjudication path for cases where the official question/answer and the exam-time MOJ article directly resolve the ambiguity.

## Trust boundary

The new verification level is `evidence_adjudicated_historical_v1`.

It means:

- this was **not** Stage 6 high-confidence machine promotion;
- this is **not** labeled human/owner review;
- the decision is reproducible from a durable snapshot of the live official-law evidence plus current tracked official question metadata and selected answer-option evidence;
- Stage 6 thresholds remain unchanged;
- Official Core is never mutated or copied into the combined verified output.

`historical_law_evidence_adjudication.py` fails closed if any of the following drifts:

- durable snapshot run/artifact provenance;
- exam code, article, article fingerprint/text markers or selected official version;
- Stage 4 evidence-ready/eligible status;
- Stage 5 promotion-candidate status;
- Stage 6 `historical_semantic_support` state, decision reason or top article;
- current law-link official answer or stem markers;
- current question-shard official answer, stem, selected answer-option evidence or MOEX source URL.

A `held` adjudication is explicitly excluded from the combined verified result.

## Long-term evidence note

The durable snapshot exists because a 14-day CI artifact is not a long-term evidence store. The original run/artifact IDs are retained as provenance, while the minimum replay evidence is versioned in Git. This does **not** authorize mirroring question Official Core or arbitrary third-party source content.

- no claim that the entire law-question corpus is historically verified;
- no automatic promotion of future semantic-support cases without an explicit evidence-adjudication record.
